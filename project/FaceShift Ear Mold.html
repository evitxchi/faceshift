§ Platform

Three steps. One pipeline.

FaceShift is the first 3D-printed predicate for customizable craniofacial molding, combining a bedside scan, a clinician-guided design process, and a same-day silicone print into a single corrective workflow.

01 · SCAN

30-second 3D scan

Captured at the bedside with a structured-light depth sensor. No anesthesia, no contact, no calibration phantom, the infant stays in the parent's arms.

02 · DESIGN

Clinician-guided correction plan

A personalized correction path is built around each infant's anatomy, each aligner gently repositioning cartilage before the next is delivered.

03 · PRINT

Same-day silicone

Printed in medical-grade silicone and sterilized for clinical use. The first aligner ships the same day as the scan.

§ Indications

Where early molding works.

Initially developed for ear deformities like lop and prominent ear, FaceShift extends to nasoalveolar molding for cleft lip and palate, jaw alignment, and other craniofacial differences, all in the soft-tissue window of the first weeks of life.

1/600

Cleft lip & palate births

The most common surgical difference in the United States. Children with cleft often face feeding, speech, and chronic ear-infection challenges, plus an average of 8–11 surgeries over childhood.

600K

U.S. infants with craniofacial differences

These conditions impact self-esteem, social development, and mental health, primarily driven by stigmatization. Early correction during the cartilage-pliable window changes the trajectory.

≤6wks

The intervention window

Soft tissue and cartilage remain pliable during the first weeks of life. Initiating molding inside this window often eliminates the need for later corrective surgery.

§ Science

Why now is the right moment.

Recent advances in non-surgical molding, plus accessible 3D printing of biocompatible elastomers, have unlocked a corrective approach that was infeasible a decade ago.

“

While infant tissues are still malleable, custom 3D-printed molds can maintain nasal structure, lower the pain of septoplasty healing, and correct cleft lip–palate, limiting the invasiveness of later surgery, or removing the need for it altogether.

§ About

Beyond surgery.

Traditional surgeries for craniofacial deformities have been costly, invasive, and often delayed, placing unnecessary burden on children and their families. FaceShift reduces that burden by making correction available earlier in life, with the potential to impact thousands of families and let children grow up without the stigma of craniofacial differences.
