Research vision

Could voice help us notice a change sooner?

We’re studying whether patterns in speech over time can add context for care teams supporting people with congestive heart failure. Earlier recognition and fewer hospitalizations are goals to investigate, not outcomes we can promise.

How we approach the research ›

Good to know.

What is SoundWell?

SoundWell is a research-stage, voice-based remote monitoring platform. Participants record short spoken phrases on a schedule set by their care or research team. The research explores whether changes in voice over time may offer useful context about heart health.

Can anyone sign up?

No. Patient access is invitation only. A member of your care or research team creates your account and sends you a six-character invite code.

How often will I record?

Your team assigns the schedule for each experiment: daily, weekly, or twice weekly. The home screen shows which sessions are due.

What happens during a session?

Open an assigned experiment, read its short phrases aloud, and record them in the app. You can play a recording back and re-record before continuing.

Can I pause before finishing?

Yes. The Exit control leaves the recording session without saving the unfinished session. Follow the instructions from your study team if you need help.

What can a care or research team do?

Teams can invite participants, create experiments with phrases and recording frequency, manage assignments, filter participant lists, and download recording data as CSV for analysis.

Does SoundWell diagnose heart failure or predict a change?

No. SoundWell is studying voice as a possible research signal. It is not presented as a diagnostic or prediction tool and should not replace medical care.
